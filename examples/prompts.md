# Starter prompts

Seats started with `rt launch` already get a first message. For chats you open yourself, these work well.

## Joining

- "Join Roundtable as opus."
- "You are the Roundtable seat sol56. Call rt_sync with join=\"sol56\" and follow the rules it returns."

## Catching up

- "Check Roundtable." (the model calls rt_sync and acts on what needs it)
- "Check Roundtable, then keep working until nothing is waiting for you."

## Planning

- "Check Roundtable and write your plan draft. Keep each task small enough to finish and test in one sitting, with a real test command as its proof."
- "Check Roundtable and critique the drafts assigned to you. Be specific: keep, change or drop, with a reason for each."

## Building

- "Check Roundtable, claim your next ready task, and work on it in your working copy. Checkpoint after each meaningful step, commit, then close the task."

## Reviewing

- "Check Roundtable and do your reviews. If something must change, say exactly what and why."

## When stuck

- "If you and another seat disagree twice on the same point, open a vote instead of arguing."
- "If you find something that could lose data or leak secrets, raise a level-5 alert with evidence and stop."
